Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 23, 2026

TruWord Music ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Do Not Collect Directly

TruWord Music does not require users to create an account, sign in, or provide personal information such as name, email address, or passwords. We do not collect directly identifiable personal information.

2. Apple Music API Usage

We use Apple’s MusicKit API to display public music data such as songs, albums, and artist information. This data is provided by Apple Music and is not personalized or directly tied to any specific user.

3. Analytics and Usage Data

We use Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google, to collect anonymous usage data such as app interactions, screen views, feature usage, and performance metrics.

This data may include device information, app usage patterns, and general interaction events (for example: song plays, searches, and feature usage). This information is used solely to improve app performance and user experience.

The data collected does not directly identify users and is processed in accordance with Google’s Firebase privacy practices.

4. No Advertising

TruWord Music does not display third-party advertisements or sell user data.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent personal information. Limited analytics data may be processed by Firebase (Google) for app functionality and performance insights.

6. Data Security

We use standard security measures and rely on Apple and Google Firebase infrastructure to help protect any processed data.
